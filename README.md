## Two decisions to make before you buy anything

**Custody.** Buy on an exchange and the exchange holds the token until you withdraw. Buy in a wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Trust) and you hold the keys, but you're swapping against on-chain liquidity and paying network fees. Neither is wrong. Exchanges are simpler for first purchases; self-custody makes sense once you plan to stake or use JUP inside Solana DeFi.

**Region.** This is the one people forget, and it's the one that wastes the most time. Gate's own guidance notes that its services may be unavailable in certain markets, explicitly including the United States, Canada, Iran and Cuba. On top of that, Gate's referral and invitation programme excludes residents of Belgium, the UK, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Turkey, Austria and South Korea. If you're in one of those places, check whether you can open a global account at all before you go through KYC.

## The mistakes that cost people the most

**Buying with a card and expecting the bonus.** Card purchases are excluded from qualifying deposits. You pay 1–5% *and* miss the reward task.

**Skipping the referral code field.** It goes in at registration or not at all.

**Sending JUP on the wrong network.** Solana only. Test with a small amount.

**Ignoring the region rules.** Opening an account you can't complete verification on wastes everyone's time.
